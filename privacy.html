Privacy policy

reels-uploader · last updated 13 October 2026

reels-uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the CABBAGE YouTube channel to upload that channel's own videos.

What it accesses

With the channel owner's permission (Google OAuth), it uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos to the owner's channel and to read that channel's basic details and video statistics.

What it stores

The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's own computer. No data about any viewer or any other user is collected, stored, sold or shared.

Third parties

Data goes only to Google/YouTube, under Google's privacy policy. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
